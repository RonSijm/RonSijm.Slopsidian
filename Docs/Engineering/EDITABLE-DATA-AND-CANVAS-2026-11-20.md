## Limits and remaining work

Bases authoring supports complete local Table/List/Cards queries and eligible properties. Warning-bearing or unsupported queries, broader fence/document/static/nested contexts, self-row authoring, additional views, row creation, full formula/type/plugin APIs, remote covers, virtualization and original/private/visual parity remain open.

Nested authoring requires proven punctuation and source spans. Aliases/tags/merge/prototype or duplicate keys, ambiguous/multiline flow layouts and other unsupported edits refuse or stay Source-only. General reordering, broader serialization/type/link widgets and full native provider behavior remain open.

Canvas exports intentionally use literal vector/plain-text cards and path/URL placeholders for referenced content. Full rendered document/media exports, jump, general virtualization, wider formats/devices/gestures/private APIs and original visual equivalence remain open. Conversion handles one text card and one absent Markdown destination in an existing folder; it is not a cross-file atomic transaction, and Undo keeps the acknowledged file. Cross-restart Undo is not claimed.

Actual screen review also leaves two nonblocking polish items open: a retired stale-export preview can show a broken-image/alt-text glyph after downloading is disabled, and Bases table headers/action labels wrap heavily in very crowded four-way split panes. The refusal message, review dialogs and narrow Apply/Cancel actions remain usable on both hosts.

Physical audio-device and broader codec acceptance, wider recovery, service-backed Sync/Publish and release/installation/update acceptance remain on the wider roadmap. This targeted matrix does not renew the entire core/plugin/release matrix. No commit, push, publication, signing, Windows policy, driver or dependency change was made.
