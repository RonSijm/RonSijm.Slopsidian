# Watching the night sky

A fictional handbook for the telescope station. This is sample content, not a published book or a recommendation.

## Reading notes

Explain how an eyepiece changes the field of view before inviting a visitor to look through it. Give the visitor time to describe what they notice.

Keep dark-adapted vision in mind when arranging signs and lamps. The teaching phrase **quiet red lantern** is also a useful Omnisearch target.
