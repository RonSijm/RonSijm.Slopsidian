# An Obsidian vault you can explore

Open, click, change something, see what happens. Everything here is fictional sample material.

> [!tip] Start with the built-in experience
> Community plugins are disabled. Explore the stock theme, links, properties, Canvas, Bases, daily notes and templates first.

## A five-minute route

1. Open [[30 Projects/Observatory]] and follow a project or meeting link.
2. Change a property, then open [[70 Data/Projects.base]] to see the same data as a table.
3. Move a card in [[60 Visuals/Project constellation.canvas]].
4. Try the checkboxes, diagram, image and footnote in [[80 Sandbox/Editing playground]].
5. Open [[40 Journal/Daily/2026-10-23]] and inspect its template and links.
