# Visitor briefing

Welcome to the shared opening evening. Choose the telescope station or the seedling station, then come back with one thing you noticed.

## Café welcome point

Meet Alex beside the amber comet lantern. Ask “What would you like to discover tonight?” before explaining how the stations work.

The lantern is a fictional prop with a distinctive name for search exercises. [[80 Sandbox/Plugin lab/Search/Telescope handover]] explains its use at the telescope; [[80 Sandbox/Plugin lab/Search/Garden handover]] covers the second station.

## Closing question

Invite each visitor to share one discovery. Record anonymous observations in the planning meeting rather than names or contact details.
