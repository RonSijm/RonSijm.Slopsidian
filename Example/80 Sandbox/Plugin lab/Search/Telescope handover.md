# Telescope handover

Alex hosts the telescope station for [[30 Projects/Observatory]].

## Equipment

Place the amber comet lantern next to the printed sky chart. Keep the eyepiece cap with the case so it can be found at closing time.

The fictional spectroheliograph label is a search target, not a claim that the sample event owns that instrument.

## Host prompts

Explain the eyepiece after asking the visitor what they can see. Keep the explanation separate from the technical equipment list.
