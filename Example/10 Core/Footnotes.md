# Footnotes

Open [[80 Sandbox/Editing playground#Footnotes]]. Switch to Reading view and follow the footnote marker. If your Obsidian version offers the Footnotes view, enable it under Core plugins and open its pane.

**Expected:** the reference jumps to the definition; the pane lists the active note’s footnote. The inline footnote remains useful in versions without that pane.
