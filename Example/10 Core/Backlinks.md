Open [[30 Projects/Observatory]] and show its Backlinks pane. Follow the linked reference from [[30 Projects/Meetings/2026-10-21 Planning]].

**Expected:** the meeting appears as a linked mention. Expand unlinked mentions to look for the plain-text mention in [[80 Sandbox/Search target]]. A link to a heading and a link to a block are included in that meeting.
