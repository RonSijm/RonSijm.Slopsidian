# Word count

Open [[80 Sandbox/Word count sample]]. Its body is `One two three four five.`. Select just that sentence in the editor and inspect the status bar.

**Expected:** the selection contains five words and 24 characters including spaces and punctuation. Whole-file totals include any surrounding Markdown and can vary by version.
