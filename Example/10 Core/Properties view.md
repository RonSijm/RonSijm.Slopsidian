# Properties view

Open [[30 Projects/Observatory]]. Inspect `status`, `priority`, `hours`, `due`, `reviewed`, `owner`, and `tags`. Change `hours` from 12 to 13.

**Expected:** Obsidian preserves their types. [[70 Data/Projects.base]] reflects the edit; Dataview can read the same properties after you enable it. Restore 12 to return to the sample baseline.
