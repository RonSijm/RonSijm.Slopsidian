# Publish

Obsidian Publish is an optional hosted service. To test it, use your own Publish subscription and explicitly select a few sample pages and their attachments.

**Expected:** selected notes appear on your chosen Publish site only after you publish them. No account, subscription, credentials or automatic publishing are supplied by this vault.
