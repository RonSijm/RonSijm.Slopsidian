# Audio recorder

First play [[90 Attachments/Synthetic tone.wav]], a generated one-second test tone. To exercise recording, choose a test microphone/input and run Audio recorder’s start/stop command while a sandbox note is active.

**Expected:** an audio attachment and playable embed are created. Recording needs microphone permission and a suitable device; the supplied tone needs neither.
