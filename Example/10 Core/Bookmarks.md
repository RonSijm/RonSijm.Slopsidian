# Bookmarks

Open the Bookmarks pane. Use the supplied **Start here** and **Project canvas** bookmarks. Add [[30 Projects/Observatory#Next actions]] from its heading menu, then move the bookmark into a group.

**Expected:** the bookmark opens the intended note or heading, and the group remains after reopening the vault.
