# Outline

Open [[80 Sandbox/Editing playground]] and show Outline. Expand the nested headings, click “Deep detail”, then rename that heading in the note.

**Expected:** navigation reaches the section and the outline follows the edit. Compare the behavior in editing and Reading view.
