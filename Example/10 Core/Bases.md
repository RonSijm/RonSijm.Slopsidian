# Bases

Open [[70 Data/Projects.base]]. Try its “All projects” and “Active projects” views. Edit a project’s `hours` or `status`, then reopen its note.

**Expected:** four project rows appear in All projects and two active rows initially appear in Active projects. The `remaining` formula subtracts `completed` from `hours`; the formula summary totals 16 at the original baseline.
